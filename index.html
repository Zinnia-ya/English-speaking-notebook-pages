今天的易错点

❌ I cooking instant noodles.
✅ I'm cooking instant noodles.
进行时需要 am。
❌ I using...
✅ I'm using...
同样要说 I am using / I'm using。
❌ I'm like it.
✅ I like it.
like 在这里不需要 am。
❌ I like a little spicy.
✅ I like it a little spicy.
加上 it 更自然。
❌ a little spices
✅ a little spicy
spicy 是“辣的”；spices 是“香料”。

Lesson 02 · 本次易错点

❌ Haven't left yet.
✅ I haven't left yet.
完整句子需要补上主语 I。
❌ We are planning to meet at around three minutes thirty.
✅ We are planning to meet at around three thirty.
表达 3:30 直接说 three thirty。
❌ We'll chat and check up.
✅ We'll chat and catch up.
catch up 表示“叙旧、聊近况”；check up 表示“检查”。
❌ Are there, we'll chat and catch up.
✅ While we are there, we'll chat and catch up.
表示“当我们在那里时”，要用 while we are there。
